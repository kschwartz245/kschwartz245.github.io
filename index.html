About Me

A snippet about me and what I do.

My name is Keegan Schwartz, I'm an aspiring game developer and designer from Wisconsin. I take pride in making ideas come to life through both the process of design and programming. With a sharp mind for thinking on my feet and outside the box, I can keep work flowing despite hiccups that threaten to slow down development of my projects. My personal passion is to learn constantly and always be improving or finding something new I haven't learned yet.

I'm currently a student at the University of Wisconsin-Whitewater, acquiring my Bachelors in Media Arts and Game Development, with a minor in general business. I have a few small projects under my belt, and I'm always looking to expand my portfolio and learn new skills.
